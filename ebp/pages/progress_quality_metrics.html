Notes: This chart shows the number of taxa at each taxonomic rank for which at least one genome assembly in GoaT meets the proxy EBP quality thresholds. The EBP standard criteria shown here reflect GoaT's relaxed interpretation of EBP assembly standards, based on available data. This assessment uses a subset of the metrics recommended in the EBP Scientific Subcommittee's Report on Assembly Standards, specifically: contig and scaffold N50, and the percentage of sequences assigned to chromosomes. Counts here therefore represent a subset of assemblies that partially fulfil EBP standards, not a fully certified assessment. See the GoaT FAQs for details on how these categories are calculated.

EBP contribution refers to taxa where at least one qualifying assembly is directly linked to the EBP umbrella (PRJNA533106). Non-EBP contribution is the remainder — taxa where the quality threshold is met exclusively by assemblies from non-EBP projects.

Data source: Genomes on a Tree (GoaT). Used with permission.
