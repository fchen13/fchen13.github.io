How to read this

The three reports do not overlap. Report 1 selects species with an EBP-standard assembly already in INSDC; reports 2 and 3 select species without one, split by whether your project has started work. So a species appears in exactly one of them, and the three counts add up to the total.

Every number counts species by your project’s sequencing status. The other project’s status for the same species is in GoaT — that is what the columns are: how many of your species each of them also holds.

These numbers are as of the capture date, not this moment. Each project’s report is built once a week from GoaT and stored; opening one reads the stored copy, which is what makes it instant — rebuilding Darwin Tree of Life’s takes 16 GoaT queries and about 37 seconds. The GoaT links on this page do open GoaT and will show you what is true there right now; the counts here do not change until the next capture.

Where duplication is not the same as waste. Two projects on one species may be a deliberate reference-quality re-sequencing, a different population, or a collaboration already under way. These reports say what GoaT records; the projects decide what to do about it.
